# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Eepy Host project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level,
gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, neurological
difference, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion,
or level of commitment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
comments, discussions, and any related channels — as well as in person when
a participant is representing the project.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community:

- Empathy and respect toward other people and their work
- Grace in accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users
- Clear communication about intent and impact

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment of any kind, and demeaning or discriminatory jokes, remarks, or
  imagery
- Personal, political, or religious attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as a home or work address,
  email, or account identifiers)
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the project maintainer. Reports can be submitted as a
private issue via the repository's issue tracker (or, for reports involving
vulnerabilities, through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
project maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
person filing the report.

Retaliation against someone who reports an incident will itself be treated
as a violation of this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of
Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when
appropriate.
